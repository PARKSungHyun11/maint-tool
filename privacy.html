Maint Tool Privacy Policy

Effective: August 12, 2026

Overview

Maint Tool is an offline-first utility for aircraft maintenance date, time, unit, dispatch calculations, and flight log drafting.

Accounts and Cloud Sync

You may use the app without cloud sync. If you create an account, Maint Tool processes your email address, account type, airline selection, subscription entitlement, and the flight log drafts you choose to synchronize. Passwords are handled by the authentication provider and are not stored as readable text by Maint Tool.

Data Stored on Your Device

Calculator history, the selected time zone, account session data, and flight log drafts are stored on your device so the app can work offline. You can delete individual drafts in the app. Removing the app removes locally stored data; synchronized drafts may remain in your account until you delete the account or the cloud copy.

Network Access

Calculations, local draft editing, and export are designed to work without Wi-Fi, LTE, roaming, or any other network connection. Account creation, first sign-in, cloud synchronization, advertisements, purchases, and subscription refresh require network access.

Subscriptions and Advertising

New accounts may receive a 14-day ad-free trial. An optional monthly subscription removes advertising. Users who do not subscribe may see a small banner advertisement when online. Subscription status is processed by Apple App Store or Google Play and RevenueCat. Advertising will be provided by the disclosed advertising provider before public release.

Export

You can export drafts as PDF, plain text, or a JSON backup through the operating system share sheet. The app does not control how the destination app stores a file after you share it.

Account Deletion

Before account features are publicly released, Maint Tool will provide an in-app option to request deletion of the account and associated synchronized data.

Changes

If the app later adds online services or data collection, this policy and the app-store privacy disclosures will be updated before those features are released.
